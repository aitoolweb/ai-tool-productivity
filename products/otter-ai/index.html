Reliable live transcription and collaborative meeting notes.

Editorial Review

Verdict

Otter.ai is a practical choice when participants need to see and work with a transcript during a meeting, interview, or lecture. Live transcription, speaker labels, comments, and highlights make the record collaborative rather than purely archival. Accuracy still depends on microphones, accents, background noise, and specialist vocabulary, so the transcript should not be treated as an authoritative record without review.

Recommended workflow

Connect the relevant calendar, but limit automatic recording to meetings that genuinely need a transcript. Add vocabulary for names and domain terms where supported. During the session, mark important moments instead of trying to correct every sentence in real time. Afterward, verify decisions and action items against the audio, assign owners, and export only the material that belongs in the team's permanent knowledge system.

Value assessment

The free plan is sufficient for testing live accuracy and basic collaboration. Paid plans make sense when monthly transcription volume, exports, search, or team features become operational requirements. Otter creates the most value for interviews, education, media, and meeting-heavy roles. It creates less value when calls are short, highly confidential, or already documented through a disciplined agenda and note-taking process.

Alternatives to consider

Fireflies.ai offers broader automation and conversation analytics for teams. Fathom provides a simpler post-meeting workflow for many individual users. tl;dv is better suited to sharing short evidence clips from recorded calls. Native transcription in Zoom, Meet, or Teams may be enough when the organization wants fewer vendors and only needs basic notes.
