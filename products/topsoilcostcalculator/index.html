Topsoil Cost Calculator is a free English-language toolset for U.S. homeowners and DIY landscapers. It estimates material volume and cost for topsoil, sod and sand in U.S. customary units, converting between cubic yards, tons, bags, rolls and pallets as needed for a planning figure. Calculations run locally in the browser — no account, download or personal data is required.

Editorial Review

Verdict

Topsoil Cost Calculator is a practical free tool for anyone estimating a landscaping order. Instead of doing yard-to-bag-to-ton math by hand, you enter the project dimensions and get a volume and cost planning figure for topsoil, sod or sand.

Recommended workflow

Choose the material you are ordering, enter the area and depth (or the units you buy in, such as bags or rolls), and the calculator returns the quantity and an estimated cost to plan against. All math happens locally in the browser.

Value assessment

It is free with no registration, and it removes the most error-prone part of a DIY landscaping project — converting between cubic yards, tons, bags and pallets. Best used as a planning estimate before confirming quotes with a local supplier.

Alternatives to consider

General construction calculators and spreadsheet math can cover the same ground with more manual setup. A local supplier's quote remains the final word; this tool is for getting into the right ballpark first.
