# Reclaim.ai

## Editorial review

### Verdict

Reclaim.ai is a strong option for people who want to defend focus time without replacing their existing calendar. Flexible blocks for tasks, habits, and breaks can move around meetings while still preserving weekly intentions. The system is less rigid than an automatically planned day, but it still requires sensible priorities and availability rules. Calendar-heavy teams will gain more than users with few scheduled commitments.

### Recommended workflow

Connect the calendars that determine real availability, then define working hours and priority rules before adding many habits. Start with recurring focus time and one or two flexible routines. Add tasks only when they need protected calendar space. Review scheduling conflicts weekly and adjust rather than overriding blocks repeatedly. Shared meeting rules can reduce fragmentation when several teammates use the product consistently.

### Value assessment

The free plan is useful for individuals testing focus-time protection and basic scheduling. Paid features are more relevant for teams, advanced controls, and broader calendar coordination. Reclaim creates value when it prevents open time from being consumed by low-priority meetings and reminders. It is less compelling when a calendar is already stable or when work cannot be planned in advance.

### Alternatives to consider

Motion provides a more comprehensive task-and-calendar system with stronger automatic day planning. Clockwise focuses on team calendar optimization and meeting patterns. A simple recurring block in Google Calendar may be enough for users with predictable schedules. Choose Reclaim when flexible protection of habits and focus time is more important than a full project manager.
