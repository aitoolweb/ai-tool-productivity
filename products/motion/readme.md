# Motion

> An intelligent calendar that automatically plans your task list.

## Overview

Motion places tasks directly on your calendar based on duration, deadlines, and priority. When plans change, it rebuilds the schedule automatically so important work still gets done.

## Editorial review

### Verdict

Motion is built for people who want tasks placed directly onto a calendar and automatically rearranged when plans change. This can reduce the daily effort of deciding what to work on next. It works best when tasks have realistic durations, priorities, and deadlines. Users who prefer a flexible list or frequently ignore time blocks may find the automated schedule more demanding than helpful.

### Recommended workflow

Begin with one workstream and add only tasks that genuinely need calendar time. Estimate duration conservatively, set hard deadlines sparingly, and protect fixed commitments before allowing automatic scheduling. Review the plan at the start and end of each day, correcting estimates so future schedules improve. Keep very small tasks grouped rather than filling the calendar with short fragmented blocks.

### Value assessment

Motion is a paid productivity system rather than a casual AI add-on. Its value depends on whether automated planning replaces a meaningful amount of manual scheduling and reduces missed deadlines. A trial should include a normal week with interruptions, not an idealized empty calendar. Compare the result with a simpler task manager and manual time blocking before paying for automation.

### Alternatives to consider

Reclaim.ai layers flexible focus time, habits, and tasks onto an existing calendar and offers a free entry point. Traditional tools such as Todoist or TickTick may suit users who prefer lists and manual control. Notion AI and Taskade focus more on project information and collaboration. Choose Motion when calendar execution, not documentation, is the central problem.
